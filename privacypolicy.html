Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

LITHRA is designed to keep your fitness data private. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, or developer-operated server. Your fitness data is stored locally unless you explicitly choose to export it, share it, or save a completed workout to Apple Health.

Information stored on your device

LITHRA stores the information you enter or generate while using the app, including routines, exercises, targets, notes, workout history, body measurements, preferences, reminder state, and training progress. While a workout is active, the app also stores temporary workout state such as the current exercise, completed sets, timers, cardio details, and an optional locker number. This information is stored locally in the app’s container and is not transmitted to us.

Live Activities

During an active workout, LITHRA can share temporary workout information locally with its Live Activity extension so that progress, exercise details, and timers can appear on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. This communication occurs through the app’s shared container on your device and does not use a remote server.

Apple Health

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration, LITHRA can write completed workouts, the associated routine name as workout metadata, and recorded walking, running, or cycling distance where applicable. LITHRA currently does not read information from Apple Health.

Apple Health access is optional and requires your explicit permission. You can manage or revoke this permission at any time through Apple Health or your device’s privacy settings. Health information is not used for advertising, marketing, tracking, or data mining, and it is not shared with us or any third party.

Notifications

If you enable workout reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iPhone Settings. We do not use a remote notification server and do not receive information about your notification activity.

Exports, imports, and sharing

The app lets you export workout history and share routine packs. A workout-history export can include routine and exercise names, dates, completion status, sets, weight, repetitions, duration, distance, and incline. A routine pack can include selected routine names, schedules, exercises, targets, planned sets, rest periods, progression settings, supersets, and notes. These actions occur only when you initiate them and choose a destination using Apple’s system sharing interface. Files you import are processed on your device. Once you share a file outside the app, the recipient or destination service handles it under its own privacy practices.

Exercise content

Exercise instructions and available exercise media are bundled with the app and are displayed locally. LITHRA does not contact an external service to download this content while you use the app.

Data collection and tracking

We do not collect personal data through the app. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, sell data, or share app data with data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains in LITHRA’s local app container until you edit or delete it, remove the app, or erase your device. Local app data may also be included in device backups according to your Apple and iCloud backup settings. Removing the app may not immediately remove copies retained in an existing device backup. Data written to Apple Health is controlled separately by Apple Health and can be viewed or deleted there.

Children’s privacy

LITHRA is a general fitness logging tool and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email support@abgw.ae. If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information only to respond to and manage your request and retain it only as reasonably necessary for that purpose.
