Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Obsidian Lift is designed to keep your fitness data private. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, or developer-operated server. Your routines and workout history stay on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share them.

Information stored on your device

Obsidian Lift stores the information you enter or generate while using the app, including routines, exercises, targets, notes, workout history, preferences, reminder state, and training progress. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Apple Health

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration, Obsidian Lift can write completed workouts and recorded cardio distance to the Apple Health app. Obsidian Lift currently does not read information from Apple Health.

Apple Health access is optional and requires your explicit permission. You can manage or revoke this permission at any time through Apple Health or your device’s privacy settings. Health information is not used for advertising, marketing, tracking, or data mining, and it is not shared with us or any third party.

Notifications

If you enable workout reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iPhone Settings. We do not use a remote notification server and do not receive information about your notification activity.

Exports, imports, and sharing

The app lets you export workout history and share routine packs. These actions occur only when you initiate them and choose a destination using Apple’s system sharing interface. Files you import are processed on your device. Once you share a file outside the app, the recipient or destination service handles it under its own privacy practices.

Data collection and tracking

We do not collect personal data from the app. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, sell data, or share app data with data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, remove the app, or erase your device. Data written to Apple Health is controlled separately by Apple Health and can be viewed or deleted there.

Children’s privacy

Obsidian Lift is a general fitness logging tool and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email support@abgw.ae.
